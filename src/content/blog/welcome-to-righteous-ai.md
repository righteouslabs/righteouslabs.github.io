Righteous AI exists for a simple reason: most teams don't need *more* AI — they
need AI that **actually ships**. A working feature in production beats a clever
prototype that never leaves the demo.

This blog is where we'll share what we learn building exactly that.

## What we care about

- **Working software over slideware.** The goal of every engagement is something
  running in production, not a slide deck.
- **Simplicity over complexity.** The best system is the smallest one that solves
  the problem.
- **Honesty about AI.** We'll use AI where it genuinely helps, and say so plainly
  where it doesn't.

## What to expect here

Short, practical notes: how we pick use cases, how we keep build processes lean,
the tools we reach for, and the occasional honest post-mortem.

If that sounds useful, [say hello](/contact). We read every message.
